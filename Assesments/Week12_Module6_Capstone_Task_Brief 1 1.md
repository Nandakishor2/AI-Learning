# **What this week is about**
---This is the final week, and there’s nothing new to learn — on purpose. You’ll combine everything from the last eleven weeks into one complete, working app, package it so a stranger could run it, and present it in a short demo that stands up to tough questions. This is your portfolio piece.
# **Why it matters**
---

| **Why nothing new in the final week?**Because the skill being tested is putting the pieces together into something real — not cramming one more topic. Integration is its own hard job. | **Why does a “flawless” demo count against me?**A demo where nothing ever fails means you’re hiding the failures. Showing a real problem and how you handle it is what proves you can do the job. | **What makes it “client-ready”?**A stranger can read your short guide and get it working without asking you anything. That’s the real test. |

# **How your mentor checks**
---**Friday · no marks**

A quick review of your task. Not a test — just a few things your mentor looks at, so you can fix anything before Monday.

- Does the whole app run end to end from a single starting point?

- Can a stranger get it working from the guide alone?

- Does the demo show a real failure and how it’s handled — not a fake-perfect run?
