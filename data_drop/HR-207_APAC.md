# HR-207: Regional Leave Policy Addendum - APAC

## 1. Scope and Applicability
This addendum governs statutory and supplemental leave for all permanent and probationary employees operating within the APAC region effective 2026-01-01.

## 2. Annual Leave Accrual
All full-time staff accrue annual leave at a standard rate of 1.75 days per completed calendar month of service.

## 3. Carry-Over Rules
### 3.1 Standard Carry-Over
Employees may roll over up to 10 unused leave days into the next calendar year. Any excess days are forfeited automatically on March 31st.

### 3.2 Probationary Employee Carry-Over Cap
| Employee Tier | Probation Status | Carry-Over Cap (Days) | Forfeiture Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 Staff | In Probation (<6 mos) | 3 | 60 days post-probation |
| Tier 2 Leads | In Probation (<6 mos) | 5 | 90 days post-probation |
| Standard | Confirmed (>6 mos) | 10 | Standard March 31st |

Employees under Section 3.2 must obtain manager approval before utilizing carry-over leave during active probation periods.
