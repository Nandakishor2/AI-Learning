# HR-208: Parental & Family Support Addendum - LATAM

## 1. Scope
Outlines family leave provisions across LATAM operating entities effective 2026-03-01.

## 2. Caregiver Provisions
### 2.1 Primary Caregiver Scale
| Tier | Continuous Service | Paid Duration | Stipend Support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Category A | < 6 months | 6 weeks full pay | Local statutory |
| Category B | 6+ months | 12 weeks full pay | 100% health continuation |

### 2.2 Paternity and Non-Birthing Support
Non-birthing parents receive 15 consecutive calendar days of paid paternity leave.
