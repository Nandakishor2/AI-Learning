# HR-208: Parental & Family Support Addendum - North America

## 1. Purpose
Defines paid and unpaid parental leave entitlements for NA employees effective 2026-01-15.

## 2. Primary Caregiver Leave
### 2.1 Eligibility Matrix
| Tenure Category | Continuous Service | Paid Leave Benefit | Unpaid Extension Allowed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early Stage | < 12 months | 8 weeks full pay | 4 weeks |
| Standard | 12 - 36 months | 14 weeks full pay | 6 weeks |
| Senior | > 36 months | 18 weeks full pay | 8 weeks |

### 2.2 Secondary Caregiver Entitlement
Secondary caregivers are eligible for 4 consecutive weeks of fully paid leave, regardless of tenure, provided they have completed 90 calendar days of service.
