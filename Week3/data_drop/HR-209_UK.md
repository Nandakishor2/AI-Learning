# HR-209: Sick & Medical Leave Policy Addendum - UK

## 1. Overview
Governs occupational and statutory sick pay (SSP) for UK-based personnel effective 2026-01-01.

## 2. Medical Certification and Caps
### 2.1 Notification Matrix
| Absence Duration | Certification Required | Full Pay Eligibility | Reduced Pay Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 3 days | Self-certification | 100% salary | N/A |
| 4 to 7 days | Self-certification | 100% salary | N/A |
| 8+ days | Statement of Fitness for Work (GP Fit Note) | 100% (up to 4 weeks) | 50% for next 8 weeks |

### 2.2 Chronic Health Support
Specialist medical consultations are approved for up to 5 paid discretionary working days per calendar year.
