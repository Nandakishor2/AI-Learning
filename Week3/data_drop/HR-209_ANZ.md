# HR-209: Sick & Medical Leave Policy Addendum - ANZ

## 1. Context
Standardizes personal and carer's leave across Australia and New Zealand effective 2026-04-01.

## 2. Personal Leave Allowances
### 2.1 Accumulation Schedule
| Service Year | Annual Personal Leave | Carer's Conversion Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 10 days fully paid | Max 5 days |
| Year 2+ | 12 days fully paid | Max 8 days |

### 2.2 Unplanned Absence Evidence
A medical certificate or statutory declaration is mandatory for personal leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days.
